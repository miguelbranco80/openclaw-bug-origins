- Reasoning: The headline hang comes from the interrupted-update recovery path added in 59834998f25 (#165863): it stops forward command scopes on a signal during verifying and runs the restart/verification inside such a scope, but the readiness poller (its signal line dates from 2b125389c8b, Sept 22, before this commit) never read the scope signal, so every systemctl spawn died and polling continued until the deadline; that feature shipped with the bug.
