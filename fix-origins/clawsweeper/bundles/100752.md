- Origin: regression (confidence high)
- Fix: fix(cron): prevent duplicate one-shot delivery after restart (#159873)
- What the user saw: A recipient could get the same one-shot cron announcement or webhook twice if the Gateway restarted after the recipient accepted the first delivery but before the Gateway recorded the acknowledgment.
- What the fix does: The fix records delivery attempts durably before sending, replays only one-shot runs whose receipts prove delivery never started, and leaves ambiguous runs disabled with an Unknown delivery status.
- What in the causing PR was wrong: The recovery diff added `recoverInterruptedOneShot: params.mode === "startup"`, allowing an interrupted one-shot to replay without checking whether the recipient had already accepted its delivery.
- Reasoning: I checked the causing commit and its parent. The parent already supported announcements and webhooks, and its startup recovery disabled interrupted one-shots and skipped catch-up. The causing commit enabled catch-up based on startup mode alone, and the fix’s documented negative control reproduced duplicate delivery for both transports with that recovery policy.
