- Fix: fix(cloud-workers): preserve replacement owners during forced abandonment (#174826)
- What the user saw: After a forced stop, a replacement cloud worker turn could be marked failed and lose its pending workspace result.
- What the fix does: The fix captures placement and turn ownership before cleanup, checks it again during state transitions, and preserves replacement workspace results and journals.
- What in the causing PR was wrong: The change to `const journalOwners = (await params.placements.listWorkspaceReconciliationOwners()).filter(` let forced abandonment pause before selecting pending results and placements, so it could resume and tear down a replacement owner.
- Reasoning: I checked the parent of #161539 and confirmed that journal preparation and pending-result selection were synchronous, so an active turn with no pending result could not be replaced during those reads. The journal refactor introduced a yield before result selection, and the fix's tests reproduce the same stale-owner failure during the later asynchronous pending-result read. The subsequent move to asynchronous placement transitions added a separate window for same-generation claim replacement.
