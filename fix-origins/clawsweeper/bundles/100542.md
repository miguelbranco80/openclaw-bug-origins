- Reasoning: The introducing commit bundled the routing roster and settled rail view into one snapshot, so an oversized roster could block the entire snapshot and a completed Pages read could still wait on the inactive Sessions roster. I checked that the predecessor cache also rejected rosters over 200 rows and that the later schema extraction preserved the same cap, so the evidence supports defects present when the unified snapshot shipped.
