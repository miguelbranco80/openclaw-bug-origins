## The bug (from the fix-origins reader)
- Origin: never-worked (confidence high)
- Fix: fix(ui): let each page decide its sidebar (#199831)
- What the user saw: Usage and some plugin pages could retain an unrelated sidebar, the closed More menu could look active, and opening Automations could briefly flash its old list.
- What the fix does: The fix derives sidebar visibility from the active page, ties the More highlight to the open menu, and lets Automations know before its first frame whether the sidebar is visible.
- What in the causing PR was wrong: The new route observer changed the sidebar only under `if (section && route !== previous && owner.navigationView !== section)`, so routes without a section could retain the previous sidebar.
- Reasoning: I checked #199825 and its parent: the parent used Pages as a sidebar view, and #199825 introduced section-specific sidebars and the More popover. That first implementation handled sidebar-less destinations in rail and menu click handlers but left them unhandled by the route observer, and it styled More from the active route even when the popover was closed. The Automations flash came from a separate layer, #199827, which registered sidebar presence in `onSettled`.
