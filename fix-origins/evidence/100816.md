## What Problem This Solves

Fixes repeated Telegram round-trip checks being rejected or sent to the wrong conversation after a selected direct-message, channel, or forum-topic scenario.

## User Impact

QA operators can repeat latency checks on the conversation and topic actually exercised by the selected scenario, using the provisioned primary participant. There is no change to normal Telegram chat behavior or credential handling.

## Why This Change Was Made

Capture the QA bus cursor before each scenario attempt and resolve the probe route from that attempt's surviving inbound or outbound messages for the active account. This covers scenarios without a single declared conversation, including outbound-only flows, without parsing scenario actions or maintaining another route catalog.

The first probe is fresh; later probes reply only to their own preceding responses. A response from another topic fails the probe instead of counting as a latency sample. Explicit-input probes remain supported, and native observer admission guards are unchanged.

This reworks Vincent Koc's original PR against the current QA lifecycle while preserving its branch and history. The original stays open until this canonical replacement is verified merged.
