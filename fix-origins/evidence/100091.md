## Fix PR #159370 description (by steipete, opened 2026-09-27T03:15:37Z)

```text
## What Problem This Solves

Fixes Doctor skipping legacy plugin capture cleanup when macOS denies access to another account's process arguments.

Closes #159112. Thanks @bdjben for the NoMachine, Logitech, and softwareupdated reproduction evidence.

## User Impact

`openclaw doctor --fix` can reclaim eligible captures despite unreadable arguments from foreign-UID processes. Same-UID or unknown-UID uncertainty still preserves legacy captures. Doctor also preserves foreign-owned capture directories, including when run with elevated privileges.

Managed native cleanup uses recorded custody and installed-index references without a host-wide argv veto. Cleanup failures remain advisory. No configuration, CLI options, dependencies, database schemas, or update snapshot/rollback behavior change.

## Why This Change Was Made

The Darwin reader previously required a narrow executable-path and Apple-signature fallback after `KERN_PROCARGS2` failed. Third-party daemons and Apple `.app` services fell outside it and blocked the complete Doctor census. The reader now uses the observed UID boundary and records the exclusion once at debug level. Calls without a supplied UID, including Gateway lock inspection, stay conservative.

The legacy deletion owner rechecks directory ownership alongside current identity and maintenance authority. This prevents a privileged Doctor from deleting another user's legacy capture after excluding that user's unreadable process. Managed native captures keep the existing custody and loaded-module owner from #158950; no parallel holder registry is introduced.
```
