## What Problem This Solves

Fixes the restart-readiness regression introduced by #175307 without removing its revoked-caller and cancellation guarantees. When an old reply's lifecycle retires while a cold agent registration reports its already-completed COMMIT, the inner admission helper returned but the outer callback still checked caller authority. That refused a bookkeeping acknowledgement while the worker had an open connection but had not installed its identity. Settlement then replaced the expected stale-lifecycle refusal with an unknown-outcome error.

The committed-registration exception now ends the outer authorization callback. The admission observer still validates and records the exact registration receipt. The next validation and every actual operation still require live authority. First-use creation wrappers keep their independent checks, and genuine reporting failures still require retirement.

The queued-collector failure is a separate fixture assumption. Since #174111, “uncaptured lifecycle events publish current updated facts without predecessor label/swarm/capacity overrides.” The fixture selected only `reason: create`. It now selects the `sessions.changed` publication for either documented `create` or `updated` form, retaining all label, status, runtime, Stop and authorization assertions.
