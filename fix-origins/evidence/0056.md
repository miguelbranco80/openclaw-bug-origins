## Fix PR #199831 description (by vyctorbrzezowski, opened 2026-11-15T22:48:33Z)

```text
## What Problem This Solves

Pages without a list of their own (Usage, Knowledge and other plugin pages) still show a sidebar, the ⋯ item reads as current when it is not, and opening Automations flashes its old list page for a frame.

## User Impact

Each page decides its sidebar: pages without one show only the rail and hide it at once; clicking the current section returns to its start; ⋯ is active only while its menu is open; Automations and Dashboards open without the flash.

## Why This Change Was Made

Moves the which-sidebar decision to the page's route and makes the shell the owner of whether a section's sidebar stands beside its page, so the page knows before its first frame. The saved collapse preference is never changed by navigation.
```
