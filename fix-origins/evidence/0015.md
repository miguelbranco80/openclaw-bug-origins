## What Problem This Solves

`openclaw sessions cleanup` did not forward its command abort signal to the Gateway's unbounded `sessions.cleanup` RPC. During a real cleanup of 2,001 stored sessions, interrupting the base CLI produced exit 137 instead of the canonical interrupt status. Its catch could also classify an interrupted connection as an unreachable Gateway and try local cleanup; that fallback was not observed in the live proof.

## Change

Forward the existing async-work signal to `callGateway` and preserve its abort outcome before considering local fallback. No new output text, options, configuration, storage format or protocol changes. Contributor commit and credit are retained.

## Evidence

[Natural-path before/after receipts](https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw/pull/198886#issuecomment-6879426570): real isolated Gateway, identical whole-state snapshot, no proxy or injected delay. At two seconds into cleanup, base exits 137; candidate exits 130 for SIGINT and 143 for SIGTERM. Both uninterrupted and Gateway-stopped local controls prune 2,000 rows successfully. This proves CLI cancellation, not cancellation of server-side maintenance already accepted.

Focused source tests: 14 pass on candidate. The abort regression fails on base. Production +8/-1; tests net zero after reusing the existing cleanup-result fixture; no behavioral test removed.

## Risk

Two-way door: revert fully restores prior CLI behavior. Blast radius is interruption handling for Gateway-delegated session cleanup; explicit-store and dry-run paths are unchanged.
