## What Problem This Solves

Fatal renewal errors inside the state lease heartbeat Worker are discarded before exit, leaving Doctor and maintenance failures with only `state lease heartbeat exited`.

This is diagnostic coverage, **not a claimed fix for the original CI flake**. The two unexplained failures were [run 35588763964, job 106298641070](https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw/actions/runs/35588763964/job/106298641070) and [run 35589020413, job 106300350018](https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw/actions/runs/35589020413/job/106300350018). The second failed the required-Doctor-step refusal case; its detection-refusal case passed. Their 579 ms / 600 ms durations cannot be explained by ordinary expiry of the 60-second lease. Prior investigation had zero unforced reproductions.

## User Impact

When the Worker can report a fatal renewal error, heartbeat exits retain its name, message, SQLite code/extended code, attempt number, elapsed milliseconds since acquisition, acquisition timestamp, and last successful Worker renewal (or `never`). Abrupt exits also include the exit code and shared renewal timestamp. Lease admission, expiry, contention retries, cleanup, schemas, configuration, and update behavior are unchanged.

## Why This Change Was Made

The existing Worker sends its fatal error fields before teardown; the host reconstructs an Error cause and includes the details in its exit message. One additional shared-memory timestamp records committed Worker renewals even when a crash prevents an error message. Acquisition time comes from the existing grant's expiry minus its lease duration and survives heartbeat handoffs. The formatted diff adds 61 net production lines and no new owner or cache; the lease owner reuses its canonical identity type and shrinks by two lines.
