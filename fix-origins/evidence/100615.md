## What Problem This Solves

Fixes CI automation toggles jumping back and making the whole popover look like it reloads after each click.

Toggles respond immediately without adding lines or shifting the panel. Other options remain usable during a save. “Next check” details and automation links are removed; genuine errors and blockers remain visible. GitHub check and job links are unchanged.

The popup now projects each pending choice independently while keeping Gateway job identities and revisions authoritative. Background reads cannot replace newer intent, and an uncertain save is reconciled without repeating the mutation.
