## What Problem This Solves

Follow-up to #168790. When a link receives focus before its already-registered hovercard provider mounts, focus recovery used the pointer activation body. The card opened with a hover delay and stayed open after keyboard focus left it because it retained pointer intent.

## User Impact

Recovered keyboard focus now follows the same immediate activation, focus-visible rule, and dismissal path as an ordinary focus event. Pointer hovering keeps its existing delay and behavior.
