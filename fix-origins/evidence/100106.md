## What Problem This Solves

Fixes short Talk speech replies failing for administrator clients with “Talk session target was not prepared by the Gateway.” The failure was reproduced on an ESP watch after #184546.

## User Impact

Admin-scoped voice devices can speak a submitted result through their existing call. Exact connection, voice-session, agent, and storage-target ownership checks remain in force.

## Why This Change Was Made

Administrator access may bypass sharing policy, but it must still prepare the Talk transport’s session target. Use the existing Talk target resolver to preserve that distinction, without treating speech as an agent run.

## Evidence

- Live pre-fix failure reproduced after successful WebRTC negotiation; the Gateway rejected `talk.client.say` with `UNAVAILABLE` and the missing-target error.
- Both Talk-operator and administrator speech cases pass; all 9 focused Talk/authorization tests pass.

- All changed files pass formatting; the formatting follow-up passed the normal commit hook.
