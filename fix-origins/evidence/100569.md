```text
Related: #130741

## What Problem This Solves

Fixes an issue where channel directory discovery could persist conversation addresses while another operation held the agent database writer, or publish an address after its route was revoked during the wait.

## Why This Change Was Made

Queue address persistence through the existing conversation write owner and recheck route ownership after admission. Directory and transport preparation stay outside the writer.

## User Impact

Conversation discovery respects database contention and current routing, so revoked routes cannot publish stale addresses.

## Evidence

The original consumer failed three held-writer cases. The unchanged two-file repair passed five focused cases and 22 directory, route, and registry tests, all canonical checks, and independent review. The capture runtime's existing normal build and newer WAL integration proof are reused with their original source bindings; this is not a claim of a new integrated build or the overall Swarm 500 ms target.
```
