```text
## What Problem This Solves

On a phone, choosing **Actual** in the Systems desktop viewer leaves the closed selector reading **Act…**. You have to reopen it to read the size you just chose.

## User Impact

You can read the full **Actual** label without moving any toolbar controls or changing the tap target.

## Why This Change Was Made

The compact Systems toolbar now reserves the same 28 px for the chevron as its device picker. The 88 × 32 px control, the label’s leading edge, and the toolbar’s row centers stay put. The shared desktop viewer and forced-color controls are unchanged.

## Evidence

**390 px Systems:** available text width grew from 42 to 48 px. In the captured UI, the label ink exceeded the trailing text guide by 5.30 px before the change and cleared it by 0.70 px afterward. The view-only and size controls remained centered at y = 204.39 px; the toolbar retained its two rows with no horizontal overflow.
```
