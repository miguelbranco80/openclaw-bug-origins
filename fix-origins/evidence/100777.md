```text
## What Problem This Solves

Slow database reservations outside the session-write wrapper can leave operators with queue delays but no corresponding holder diagnostic. Session-write warnings also lose their structured timing fields in diagnostic events.

## User Impact

Operators can distinguish a settled reservation held for at least one second from a fast write that merely waited in the queue. The diagnostic reports fixed direct, worker, or ordered-read categories; it does not identify a unique request or claim that the hold was SQL execution.

## Why This Change Was Made

Observe the existing admission owner without changing FIFO, reentrancy, cancellation, or accepted-work settlement. Skip duplicate observations when the session wrapper owns timing, mark its reentrant writes, and preserve structured fields in both diagnostic events and file logs. The new reservation diagnostic contains no paths, session identifiers, payloads, or error text. Occupancy includes awaited work and is reported only after settlement; ordered reads across multiple stores can report multiple reservations. This adds diagnosis, not a latency cure. A separate follow-up remains for the existing message-first archive-pruning warnings in `session-history-archive-pruning-diagnostics.ts`; this change preserves their current file-log consumers.
```
