## What Problem This Solves

Fixes completed Claude CLI answers remaining invisible while native background research continues, including when raw previews and block streaming are disabled.

## User Impact

Completed answers reach the channel through normal reply policies and hooks without waiting for every continuation. Already delivered segments are not replayed; definitely unsent segments retain their recovery path. Background work keeps its existing admitted turn and permissions. This does not shorten the research or make the turn available to a different caller.

## Why This Change Was Made

Answer delivery and transport completion are separate boundaries. The runtime identifies held results, and the parser exposes completed answers to the existing reply pipeline. Stable segment identities survive early delivery, coalescing, deferred-tail recovery, and final settlement.

Review repairs also preserve independent replies with shared prefixes, suppress repeated result acknowledgments without losing earlier text, require a real terminal result, keep the complete final preview, and prevent completed answers from overtaking earlier presentation or remaining below a coalescing threshold.
