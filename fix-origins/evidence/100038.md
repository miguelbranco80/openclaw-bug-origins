Related: #151513 (startup visibility only; this does not complete the broader shimmer consolidation).

## What Problem This Solves

The dashboard's initial loading placeholders can look frozen because several themes give the shimmer highlight and placeholder fill the same color.

## User Impact

Startup and route-loading placeholders now show a gentle, visible two-second sweep in light and dark themes. Layout and loading timing stay unchanged, and reduced-motion users retain static placeholders. No configuration changes, dependencies, or migration are required.

## Why This Change Was Made

The screenshot's owner is the initial connection splash, not the static preboot fallback or chat-panel skeleton. This uses the existing transform-only skeleton animation with a translucent text-color highlight scoped to the shared startup/loading surface. Other skeleton consumers are unchanged.
