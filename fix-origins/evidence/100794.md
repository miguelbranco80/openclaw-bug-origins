## What Problem This Solves

Fixes healthy post-core plugin updates being stopped by an internal step allowance when the operator did not supply `--timeout`.

## User Impact

Plugin package work can finish without an implicit work deadline. Explicit deadlines still stop and settle the writer, and metadata probes remain bounded. Direct plugin updates and standalone install defaults retain their existing behavior.

## Why This Change Was Made

The post-core callers know whether the operator requested a deadline, but passed only their numeric internal allowance to installers. This carries that choice separately through convergence, configured-plugin repairs, transport adapters, and fallback attempts. Consent, lease ownership, cancellation, and rollback stay with their existing owners.
