## What Problem This Solves

Fixes: on phones, closing the Panels/Details sheet while a tool (Files, Browser…) or a Details page is open blanks the sheet before it slides away, so an empty grey panel animates off screen.

## User Impact

The sheet now slides out with the content it was showing, like an iOS sheet. Closing still takes effect immediately for focus, screen readers and the conversation layout.

## Why This Change Was Made

#188162 made the sheet itself animate out, but the pane owner flipped every content consumer to "closed" at the same instant, so panels and Details pages hid on the first frame of the exit. The pane owner (`ChatPaneSidePanels`) now has a short `exiting` state:

- `close()` still sets `open = false` at once, so the sheet becomes `hidden`/`inert`, focus returns, and the conversation reclaims its space immediately.
- While the sheet slides out, the presented layout and Details/page content keep treating it as shown (`open || exiting`), so the last panel or page stays painted.
- After the exit (`--motion-sheet-duration`, including a gesture release's own spring), the owner finishes the close: content hides, a transient menu destination returns to its saved destination, sheet height resets, and companion observer visibility syncs. Reopening mid-exit cancels the pending close; reduced motion and session changes skip it.

Reopening exposed a related bug on `main`: the hidden sheet's `visibility` transition started from `hidden` when it reopened, so Side chat's opening focus was refused for that frame (a reopen after the sheet had fully closed never focused Side chat; main's E2E only passed because it reopened mid-exit). The sheet is now visible at once on open and stays visible until its exit ends (`visibility 0s` on open, the exit duration on close). Reopening mid-exit also counts as a new Side chat opening.

This replaces the launcher-only CSS rule from #188162 (−8 lines of CSS). Minified builds serialize the token as `.441s`, so the duration parser accepts both `s` and `ms`.
