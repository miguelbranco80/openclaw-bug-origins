## What Problem This Solves

Fixes an issue where users choosing a team during guided onboarding could pass a provider check but never get their team, or get all four agents while setup still failed to complete. Interrupted setup could also lose the selected coordinator or reject a complete team whose final workspace settings had not yet been written.

## Why This Change Was Made

First-run provider activation keeps runtime metadata in defaults without claiming an agent roster. The existing model-policy migration preserves both unrestricted model selection and a newly approved model in legacy allowlists. Team name collisions and unexpected existing rosters produce explicit failures.

The existing version-1 setup receipt records the normalized coordinator ID. Setup and recovery validate that coordinator, the complete preset roster, delegation, and each member's workspace against the approved parent directory. The receipt remains authoritative across restart, including activation that needs no config write. Recovery reuses the already-approved workspace rather than requiring a later global-default write to have completed. Single-agent workspace equality is preserved.

Consent persistence, terminal handoff, and test initialization were consolidated into their existing owners. No config option, table, schema-version bump, or dependency was added. CHANGELOG.md remains release-owned.

## User Impact

A failed provider check creates no team members. Successful activation can provision the requested team, and interrupted setup resumes its saved coordinator before or after member creation. Choosing Skip still provisions the offline team and explains that provider setup remains necessary. A damaged recorded team cannot silently complete as a single-agent setup or open an invalid coordinator chat.

Existing per-member creation failures can retain members and still require manual repair; this does not introduce automatic adoption or rollback of partial teams. Legacy receipts without a recorded coordinator retain complete-layout recovery detection.
