### What Problem This Solves

On `gemini-3.8-live` and `gemini-3.8-live-extended-thinking`, a client that sends exact-zero audio when the user stops talking never gets a reply. After 500 ms of exact-zero frames, `GoogleRealtimeVoiceBridge.sendAudio` sends `audioStreamEnd` and drops further silent frames. Gemini 3.1 treats `audioStreamEnd` as the end of the user's turn. Gemini 3.8 ignores it, and its server VAD only detects the end of speech while trailing audio keeps arriving. So the turn never completes, no reply comes, and the user's transcript shows up only when they speak again, merged into that next utterance.

### User Impact

Talk and other Google realtime voice clients on Gemini 3.8 Live get replies even when their microphone sends digital-zero silence: a muted or gated microphone, a digitally silent audio leg, or a synthetic or headless driver. Ordinary microphones were not affected, because their noise floor never counts as silence. Gemini 3.1 and older models keep the existing audio-stream-end behavior.

### Why This Change Was Made

Isolated on the raw Live API on 2026-09-19, with the same speech in every run ([README](https://github.com/saariuslystoned/openclaw/blob/proof/gemini38-input-transcripts-20260919/proof/audio-stream-end-silence/README.md), [probe](https://github.com/saariuslystoned/openclaw/blob/proof/gemini38-input-transcripts-20260919/proof/audio-stream-end-silence/probe/probe_input_tx.py)):

| after speech, the client… | 3.8 Live | 3.8 Live Extended Thinking | 3.1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| keeps sending zero frames | 3/3 turns complete | 3/3 | – |
| sends 500 ms of zeros, then `audioStreamEnd`, then nothing (the bridge today) | **0/3** | **0/3** | 3/3 |
| sends 500 ms of zeros, then nothing | **0/3** | **0/3** | – |

On 3.8 the stall comes from stopping the audio stream, whether or not `audioStreamEnd` is sent. The fix is therefore a model contract: a new `endsTurnOnAudioStreamEnd(model)` (false for 3.8). When it is false, `sendAudio` forwards every frame and never ends the stream. On 3.8 the bridge now streams silence the way a real microphone already does. The two post-send `if (!silent)` branches are merged, which keeps the over-cap provider file from growing.
