## What Problem This Solves

Fixes: Android's Gateway picker closes when a finger swipe reaches or starts at the top of its saved-Gateway list.

## User Impact

Users can scroll the picker without dismissing it or changing Gateways. Back, outside-tap dismissal, and the explicit accessibility dismiss action remain available. Gateway-scoped drafts and attachments, native-window ownership, and terminal lifecycle revocation are unchanged.

The installed API-36 app now passes the native touch/IME/lifecycle test. The earlier Robolectric captures remain labeled separately.

## Why This Change Was Made

Material 3's default nested-scroll/drag handling takes unconsumed list movement and settles the sheet to `Hidden`. The original touch reproduction invoked Material's `onDismissRequest`; temporary diagnostics showed no fold-owner rejection. This change disables sheet gestures only for the Gateway picker and preserves the existing handle's explicit accessibility dismissal action, which Material otherwise removes with gestures disabled.

The picker, `FoldAwareSheet`, registry, and connection/draft owners are not replaced. There are no timers, automatic reopening, or relaxed lifecycle checks. Current iOS uses a native Menu/inline Picker; its separate draft policy is intentionally not copied to Android. Related open Android Gateway PRs did not implement this gesture fix.
