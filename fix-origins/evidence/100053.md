```text
Related: #161467

## What Problem This Solves

Fixes Gmail forwarding staying down after a Gateway restart briefly overlaps another listener on the watcher's port. Thanks to @kazuyuki-eguchi for the report and restart trace.

## User Impact

The Gateway-managed Gmail watcher recovers automatically when the conflicting listener releases its port within the bounded retry window. A persistent conflict still stops retries and now reports an error with recovery guidance. No configuration or stored-data changes.

## Why This Change Was Made

Instead of permanently stopping on the first bind error, the existing respawn lifecycle retries after 5, 10, and 20 seconds using the shared backoff helper. Shutdown cancels pending retries, ordinary exits keep their existing restart policy, and watch renewal continues if the bind retries are exhausted. This preserves the protection against endless duplicate-start loops described in #65042.

## Evidence

- Built baseline and candidate through `pnpm openclaw` and ran real isolated Gateways with a local fake `gog` executable (real TCP bind; no Google account or model requests).
- Started the watcher successfully, stopped the Gateway, held the watcher port with a local listener across replacement Gateway startup, observed `EADDRINUSE`, then released the port after two seconds. Baseline remained down (`Connection refused`); candidate retried after five seconds and returned `fixture watcher alive` over HTTP.
- Persistent-port control: candidate retried after 5/10/20 seconds, emitted `gog serve failed to bind after 3 retries`, and made no further attempts during an additional 25-second observation. All proof Gateways stopped cleanly.
- Regression failed on baseline (expected replacement child, none spawned) and passed on candidate. Focused watcher and foreground Gmail service suites: **31 tests passed**, 30.20 seconds wall including preparation. New/changed bind cases took 2–4 ms. Covers retry cancellation, split/truncated stderr markers, bounded retries, and continued renewal.
- Real Gmail registration/delivery is not exercised: the local fake replaces the external `gog` service, not Gateway lifecycle code.

### Review follow-up

The foreground CLI reference now describes the same bounded retries and continued renewal as the shared watcher service.

Recovery is intentionally bounded to three retries over approximately 35 seconds; a listener that outlasts that window requires operator intervention, as the persistent-port control demonstrates. This patch does not claim that every stale listener exits within that window or fix the earlier shutdown exception in the report.
