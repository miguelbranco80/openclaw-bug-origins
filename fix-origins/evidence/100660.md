## Fix PR #152498 description (by roboclaw-bot, opened 2026-09-19T04:40:34Z)

```text
Related: #141476, #151401

## What Problem This Solves

In **Show all agents** mode, Home appears twice and session identity icons move to the trailing edge, mixing with status indicators. Moving them back exposed an overly tight icon-to-title gap.

## User Impact

Click an agent's name or avatar to open Home, with selection and Home activity shown on the header instead of a duplicate session row. Session icons stay on the left with an 8 px gap before the title; status and actions stay on the right. Child conversations and their load/retry controls remain reachable, including through hidden subagent runs.

## Why This Change Was Made

Reuse the sidebar's canonical Home identity and session-tree projection for both navigation modes. Keep Home-owned state separate from promoted persistent descendants so activity and workspace conflicts are not counted twice. Preserve alias-equivalent Home keys and materialization keys for lazy descendants rather than only hiding a row in the renderer.

No configuration, storage, or permission changes.

## Evidence

- **523 focused tests pass** across the sidebar component, session tree, and navigation logic. Regressions were observed failing before their repairs.
- Five production-bundled Control UI Chromium cases pass with a deterministic mock Gateway. They check Home-header navigation, all-agent groups, filtering, collapse persistence, and at least 8 px icon-to-title spacing across narrow/wide, light/dark, and pointer/touch layouts.
- UI and UI-test typechecks, focused lint, stylesheet lint, formatting, and the source line-cap gate pass. Archived-child visibility, parent-owned ancestry, category uniqueness, retry recovery, and directly opened Home sessions outside the roster window have sidebar-boundary regressions.
- Independent P0–P2 review completed; actionable findings are resolved. A category-duplication concern was checked against scoped-root deduplication and eight passing chip/team, cached/loaded, categorized/uncategorized cases.
- Full backend validation was not completed locally; required exact-head CI remains a landing gate. This proof does not claim a live Gateway deployment.
```
