## Fix PR #179021 description (by vyctorbrzezowski, opened 2026-10-21T04:45:01Z)

```text
Closes #179017

## What Problem This Solves

In mobile chat, Scroll to latest can obscure an activity pill. With an expanded multiline draft, tapping it can also collapse the composer and move the button before its click activates.

## User Impact

The button rises only when a pill intersects its centered position. It stays in its usual position with no pills or a short left-only pill, and remains usable while editing a multiline draft.

## Why This Change Was Made

The existing transcript geometry owner now consumes activity-strip resize/scroll notifications and reuses cached pill bounds during ordinary transcript updates. The same owner publishes the needed clearance. The actual latest button also participates in the existing mobile composer interaction boundary, preventing premature collapse on pointerdown.

## Evidence

The original bundled UI failed the overlap assertion at 360 and 430 px. An original-source pointer trace also reproduced the expanded-composer missed click. The repaired 360 px geometry/tap case passed; the final 430 px case passed all three pill states, removal/restoration, and real taps with an eight-line draft at 430 × 560. Final 430 case: 4.418 seconds; Vitest total: 13.04 seconds. The 360 case was intentionally filtered from that final interaction replay; its geometry inputs did not change.

Existing activity tests passed 4/4; existing mobile composer tests passed 2/2 (3.00 seconds wall). UI/E2E types, changed interaction lint, E2E lint, stylelint, controller guards, formatting, and diff checks passed.
```
