## Fix PR #194684 description (by steipete, opened 2026-11-07T16:24:29Z)

```text
## What Problem This Solves

Opening `/settings` or a relocated Settings section briefly rebuilt the outgoing page before replacing it. Heavy outgoing pages such as LobsterDex paid to recreate content that immediately retired.

## User Impact

Settings links keep the outgoing page mounted until the real destination is ready. Destinations, query/hash handling, browser history, native Settings, and native navigation interception retain their existing behavior.

## Why This Change Was Made

Apply the existing Settings redirect policy at the application's pre-match canonicalization boundary. The raw router loaders retain the same fallback policy. No cache, scheduling state, production diagnostics, dependency, or lazy boundary is added.

## Evidence
```
