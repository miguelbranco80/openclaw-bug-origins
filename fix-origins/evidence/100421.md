## What Problem This Solves

Fixes fresh CLI turns forgetting the conversation after successful Gateway `sessions.compact`, including stateless `sessionMode: "none"` backends.

## User Impact

The next fresh CLI turn receives the saved compaction summary and retained recent history once, instead of receiving no history. Unknown or mixed-account history remains ineligible for automatic recovery. No configuration or schema migration is required; this does not relabel already-unknown history as trusted.

## Why This Change Was Made

Compaction writes thinking/model bookkeeping before its summary, outside the original CLI writer. The [existing boundary check](https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw/blob/25933024a1e197fa1a503e84b573a47203fc674f/src/agents/cli-runner/history-boundary.ts#L303-L312) then rejects the changed transcript watermark and stores an unknown boundary.

The CLI history owner now captures a fully covered source before compaction and re-anchors its unchanged account provenance after successful same-transcript compaction and maintenance. The commit checks the original session, writer, lifecycle, provenance and current transcript watermark. It preserves the exact admitted storage target, including legacy session-store markers. There is no alternative replay path or relaxation of the account check.

This restores the documented [CLI recovery contract](https://docs.openclaw.ai/gateway/cli-backends#sessions) and [compaction view](https://docs.openclaw.ai/concepts/compaction#how-it-works): a saved summary plus retained recent messages, not a second copy of raw pre-compaction history.
