```text
## What Problem This Solves

Opening or navigating a picker emits Solid's `STRICT_READ_UNTRACKED` warning because its scrolling effect rereads the highlighted item outside the tracking phase.

## User Impact

Keyboard selection, scrolling, search, focus, and menu layout stay the same. The picker no longer produces this reactive-read warning.

## Why This Change Was Made

Use the open state and highlighted value already captured by the effect's compute phase. Search and row changes still trigger scroll and fade updates. No new state, effect, listener, dependency, or eager import is added.
```
