Closes #151378

## What Problem This Solves

Long identity names in the sidebar are clipped without a way to reveal the hidden text.

## User Impact

Overflowing account, agent, and workspace names have a short fade and scroll to the end and back on hover or keyboard focus. Tapping keeps the existing menu interaction. Short names remain static; reduced motion disables the animation and the full name remains accessible.

## Why This Change Was Made

The three identity rows share the avatar/name/menu pattern. They now use the existing measured marquee owner with a 20px fade, 300ms initial delay, and 35px/s CSS loop. Only actual overflow is faded. The trailing fade disappears at the endpoint, Arabic names move in the appropriate direction, and hidden drawer names stop animating.
