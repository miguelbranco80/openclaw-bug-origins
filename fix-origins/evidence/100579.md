Related: #154044

## What Problem This Solves

Progress refresh can abort during hidden session preparation. Retrying a canceled queued refresh can also replay its old acceptance without starting an update.

## User Impact

Hidden status refreshes can prepare their session without becoming visible chat turns, and canceled queued requests can be retried with a new intent. Existing authorization, cancellation, replacement and terminal-run fences remain enforced. No UI layout, configuration, schema or dependency changes.

## Why This Change Was Made

This is the six-file correction for the two later review findings on #154044, which merged while these repairs were being verified. Hidden presentation is no longer mistaken for loss of admission liveness. Queued cancellation records its canonical receipt while the exact owner still exists, before removal; stale owners cannot overwrite successors.
