## Fix message body

```text
A supervisor cleanup failure recorded hours before shutdown (Team: exec service-child cleanup deadline miss at 05:14Z) made Gateway close skip database-admission stop and spawn-broker close since #188891. The still-referenced broker child kept the event loop alive until the 85 s deadline and the hard-exit watchdog SIGKILL (Team r252 stop, 07:20Z).

Gateway close now joins the supervisor first and always retires database admission, read-only workers and the broker afterward, aggregating failures. A supervisor extinction failure is reported as retained resources: an external stop exits 0 promptly while in-process restart stays refused.
```

On Linux the Gateway closes its spawn broker after core shutdown. An idle
pooled SQLite read-only session, kept for reuse after its request scope

attached-tree TERM/KILL grace (GRACEFUL_CANCEL_TIMEOUT_MS, 5 s) on a reader

sessions through their normal IPC close, reusing the idle-timer retirement
path, and fences new reads on that broker. In-flight reads are not joined:
a hung reader keeps the broker's bounded termination grace. The Gateway
always closes the broker afterwards, even if a reader close fails. The pool
moves to its own module to stay under the file-size limit.
