## What Problem This Solves

A steer send acknowledged in 51 ms later logged `slow chat send 476459ms stage=steer ... preparation=476386ms`, even though most of that interval was a full agent turn. This sent operators looking for a roughly 476-second preparation stall that had not occurred.

## User Impact

Operators can distinguish prepared-reply execution from preparation in slow chat-send logs and diagnostic events. This changes diagnostics only; acknowledgement, admission, dispatch, steering, and behavior-controlling timings remain unchanged.

## Why This Change Was Made

The existing dispatch timing scope remains in `preparation` while `reply.run_prepared_reply` runs. The diagnostics owner now switches that scope to `run` for the existing span and back to `dispatch` afterward. Detail spans retain their inclusive durations, and startup/queued diagnostics still stop at their existing boundaries. The logging guide documents the boundary and `chat.send.run` event.

The simplification pass retained the existing owners and removed no execution machinery. The necessary growth is 18 production lines and 66 test lines, covering the reported attribution bug, structured event, and startup/queued cutoff contracts.

## Evidence

With this fix it reports `preparation=14ms`, `run=476382ms`, `dispatch=20ms`, and emits `chat.send.run` with the same duration. Detail-span durations remain intact.
