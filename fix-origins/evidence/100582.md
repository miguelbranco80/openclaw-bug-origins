## Fix PR #157118 description (by steipete, opened 2026-09-24T07:23:32Z)

```text
## What Problem This Solves

Every `openclaw <command>` invocation renamed its own process to `openclaw-<command>` (`openclaw-cron`, `openclaw-status`, and even `openclaw-gateway` for `openclaw gateway call`). In process listings that reads as a fleet of differently named daemons, and it collided with the one title that is meant to be distinct: the long-running Gateway, which run-loop.ts sets to `openclaw-gateway` so lock readers and operators can tell it apart from ordinary commands.

## User Impact

`ps` shows `openclaw` for CLI commands and `openclaw-gateway` for the Gateway only. No behavior change beyond the title.

## Why This Change Was Made

Requested by the maintainer after tracing "openclaw-cron" processes on the Team host: the per-command title hid that they were plain CLI runs. The Gateway keeps its distinct title; the CLI stops inventing new ones.

## Evidence

- `src/cli/program/preaction.test.ts` now asserts the title is set to `openclaw` and never to `openclaw-status`.
- Focused run: `node scripts/run-vitest.mjs src/cli/program/preaction.test.ts` (see CI for the full lanes).
```
