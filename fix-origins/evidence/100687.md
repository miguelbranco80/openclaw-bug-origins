## What Problem This Solves

Fixes recovery discovery hiding healthy captures, and standalone Doctor retirement deleting interrupted captures, when publication stops after creating the destination but before removing the source name.

Closes #166246. Closes #166466.

## Why This Change Was Made

Main already received [fs-safe 0.24.2](https://github.com/openclaw/fs-safe/releases/tag/v0.24.2) through #166457. Both pre-repair publication sites now call fs-safe directly, removing the duplicate unsupported-rename adapter introduced by #166425. Native `auto` owns the exclusive link/unlink fallback; `require` remains fail-closed.

A remaining `manifest.json.partial` means publication is incomplete. Discovery lists it beside healthy captures without granting a sealed manifest reference; baseline reuse and forward-resolution validation refuse it; automatic retirement retains it. This applies equally to exact hard-link pairs, copied finals from the earlier adapter, and contradictory names. No name is removed and no pair is accepted as a restore point. Snapshot/payload pairs without a manifest remain incomplete evidence. Existing manifest digests, scope, directory identity, outcome binding, payload integrity, and hard-link rejection remain in force.

## User Impact

Doctor can retain pre-repair captures on Linux filesystems that reject `RENAME_NOREPLACE`, using the upstream fallback. Interrupted captures remain available for manual inspection instead of disappearing from discovery or being deleted after 30 days. Ordinary expired sealed standalone captures keep their existing retirement policy. No configuration, database schema, or capture format changes are needed.

Thanks @MertBasar0 for the reproduction and recovery-window analysis.
