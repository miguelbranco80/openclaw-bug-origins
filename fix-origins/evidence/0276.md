## Fix PR #203623 description (by roboclaw-bot, opened 2026-11-22T19:56:04Z)

```text
## What Problem This Solves

Featured templates could truncate both their name and publisher beside Add. Similarly named templates were hard to distinguish before opening one.

## User Impact

People can see the complete template name and publisher without losing the Add control.

## Why This Change Was Made

Let the name and publisher reflow inside the template row and follow the configured text size. Keep short rows unchanged when they already fit.

## Evidence

At 140% text, three of four featured rows clipped the publisher on the phone and landscape tablet. The repaired rows showed the full publisher. Four original phone rows were pixel-identical at default text. Selecting similarly named results, opening and closing files, returning focus and expanding plugin details were checked with synthetic data across four widths.
```
