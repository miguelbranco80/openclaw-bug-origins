## Fix message body

```text
Worker sessions_send resolved raw (non agent-prefixed) global family keys against the default agent's store, so a non-default agent's worker could not reach its own parent or sibling sessions. Raw lineage keys now resolve within the sending agent's store unless a fixed store owns them; explicitly qualified keys and every existing check (exact live session, lineage, visibility and send-only policy) are unchanged.

Fixes #138234
```
