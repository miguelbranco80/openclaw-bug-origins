```text
## What Problem This Solves

Fixes microphone capture starting after a push-to-talk hold has ended, right Option remaining active when left Option is still held, and stale voice transitions acquiring or releasing a newer session's resources.

## User Impact

- Releasing physical right Option ends the hold even while left Option remains down. Disabling push-to-talk cancels pending or active capture.
- Ordinary release retains the existing 1.5-second Speech drain and send; forced teardown cancels without forwarding.
- Talk suppresses push-to-talk through admission and audio shutdown. Overlay dismissal cannot release another voice owner's wake pause.
- A fresh hold can adopt visible text without forwarding the replaced capture twice.

## Why This Change Was Made

The AppState-owned graph binds PTT, Wake, Talk, session coordination, overlay actions, and acquired cleanup collaborators at construction. Hotkey invalidation is synchronous on MainActor; asynchronous startup and cleanup remain bound to the originating hold, lifecycle, owner, and acknowledged wake pause. Shared accessors project the production graph rather than creating disconnected owners.

The October 1 refresh preserves the original PR ancestry and incorporates main `55bbbf9ddcad46c7746ef8c66c73c5c71197406f`. It retains main's consolidated session source and audio shutdown/reconfiguration checks, routes new overlay-edit and Talk callbacks through the injected owner graph, and uses the current ear-boost state instead of removed helper methods. The lifecycle test now verifies edited overlay text reaches the owning coordinator and send action. Documentation describes the visible overlay lifetime without the obsolete helper reference.

No configuration, defaults, persistence, schema, protocol, or dependency changes. Microphone-selection semantics are unchanged; PCM append is not moved onto MainActor.
```
