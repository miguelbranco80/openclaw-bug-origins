## What Problem This Solves

Fixes: a recovered or promoted conversation keeps a sidebar failure warning because an old child run failed.

## User Impact

The sidebar reflects each conversation’s own failures, not historical child failures. Live delegated work, unread activity, approvals, questions, and agent-declared attention remain visible. Individual child failures remain available in their transcripts, and persistent child conversations retain their own failure indicators. No configuration or migration is required.

## Why This Change Was Made

Stop inheriting terminal failure attention and counts at the existing sidebar tree-summary owner, including cached sidebar restoration. Remove the superseded child-failure count, label, styling, and copy paths rather than adding dismissal state or another timer.

Requested by @steipete.
