## What Problem This Solves

Fixes stale parent-session attribution and permanently retained lineage lookups when many Control UI sessions are created, replaced, or removed.

## User Impact

Agent and chat events follow current prepared controller and parent-session facts. Deleted sessions, closed incognito stores, and disposed projections no longer retain a cached parent. Optional enrichment can be absent while its owner prepares facts. No protocol, configuration, or SQLite schema changes are required.

## Why This Change Was Made

The event handler used a permanent map for both successful and missing lineage lookups. Its initial lookup could also read SQLite on the Gateway thread. Events now consume the session projection owner and writer-published incognito metadata without materializing rows or rebuilding registry indexes during ingestion. Stored lineage remains canonical even when display aliases differ.

The lineage reader lives with existing projection relation reads; the sharing-entry constructor moved beside its type to keep both modules within their line caps. Production grows by 29 lines because preserving canonical selection and private-store lifetimes needs more than deleting the obsolete map.
