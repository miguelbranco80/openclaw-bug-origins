```text
## What Problem This Solves

A failed first portal-list request shows start-a-portal instructions alongside the error and offers no Retry. Portals also needs to present asynchronous reads and close operations through the native Solid 2 primitives without moving request authority out of its existing owner.

## User Impact

The first list failure now shows a working Retry action. While retrying, the page shows Loading; after a successful list, refreshes and same-client reconnects retain the selected preview and its iframe document. Rejected close operations restore the close control and keep the preview visible.

## Why This Change Was Made

The plain owner exposes its admitted list receipt and a separate first-success fact; its existing `loading`/`loaded` facts remain authoritative for `ui.command.result` acknowledgements. Native Loading/Errored boundaries observe that receipt. A scoped generator action supplies optimistic close presentation while the owner keeps permissions, admission, settlement, ordered events, selection, polling, and reachability diagnosis.

Gateway retirement releases both read and action presentation. Native target or hidden intent releases held action presentation without pretending an admitted first read has completed. The existing keyed rows, iframe key, route import, context, host properties, and ResizeObserver remain with their current owners.
```
