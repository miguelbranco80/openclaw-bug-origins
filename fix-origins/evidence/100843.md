## What Problem This Solves

Fixes an issue where a managed Codex conversation fails while refreshing its configuration because native teardown is still in progress. Treating that rejection as an unavailable thread can also rotate a valid conversation instead of preserving its history.

Observed failure: [native settled-turn finalizer, preserveNativeModel-only host profile](https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw/actions/runs/38945363873/job/117124255160): `thread/resume` returned `-32600`, reporting that the thread was still closing.

## Why This Change Was Made

Managed asynchronous teardown now waits for the exact thread's `thread/closed` notification. The earlier `notLoaded` status does not establish completed teardown. Initially unloaded threads retain their fast path; external native synchronous reloads retain their existing `notLoaded` contract.

The lifecycle owner permits one identical resume replay after the exact native pre-admission closing rejection, within the existing two-second close budget and the same signal, authority, and reservation. A second closing rejection preserves the binding, history, and siblings. Other RPC errors and ambiguous dispatched outcomes keep their existing cleanup behavior; compaction receives safe closing-error classification without a new replay flow.

Personally inspected Codex 0.162.0 at `c1382380de69521303b416720a52f42d51af6248`:

- [Pending-unload resume rejection](https://github.com/openai/codex/blob/c1382380de69521303b416720a52f42d51af6248/codex-rs/app-server/src/request_processors/thread_processor.rs#L3650-L3659), repeated after resolving history at lines 3763–3773.
- [Asynchronous close publication before clearing the pending guard](https://github.com/openai/codex/blob/c1382380de69521303b416720a52f42d51af6248/codex-rs/app-server/src/request_processors/thread_lifecycle.rs#L475-L484), and pre-subscription rejection at lines 714–726. The close notification alone is not claimed to be a race-free readiness acknowledgment.
- [Synchronous idle override reload](https://github.com/openai/codex/blob/c1382380de69521303b416720a52f42d51af6248/codex-rs/app-server/src/request_processors/thread_processor.rs#L4351-L4380) calls `finalize_thread_teardown` at lines 1007–1017, which emits `notLoaded` without `thread/closed`.

## User Impact

Managed Codex conversations can refresh configuration while preserving existing history and unrelated work. If teardown remains incomplete, the original actionable closing error is retained. No configuration, dependency, protocol, persistence, or timeout changes.

Production: +104/−28 lines (net +76), justified by the native close/admission contract and ownership-preserving error handling. Tests/support: +153/−24 lines (net +129).
