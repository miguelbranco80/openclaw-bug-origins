```text
## What Problem This Solves

Fixes: suggested-task launch buttons can show dark text on their accent background, and their options menu opens over the card instead of below the button.

## User Impact

User impact: the launch label and chevron stay off-white and readable, including with bright custom accents, and the menu opens downward without covering the suggestion. Existing launch choices, keyboard dismissal, and edge-of-viewport repositioning are unchanged.

## Why This Change Was Made

The suggestion control pairs its off-white foreground with a lightness-limited accent background and darkens hover/focus states to preserve contrast. It also requests bottom-aligned menu placement. Requested by @steipete.

- Chromium mocked-Gateway E2E: all 7 scenarios pass, including all three launch modes, white accents in light/dark themes, saturated green, Escape focus restoration, busy/read-only states, and stacked suggestions. Both button halves meet 4.5:1 contrast in normal and hover states.
- The added assertions failed before the fix for dark button text and upward menu geometry. Bright-accent contrast assertions also failed on the initial PR head, then passed after the background repair.
- Suggestion rendering/lifecycle unit tests: 11 pass across 2 files.
- Changed-file checks pass: formatting, core-test and UI typechecks, targeted lint/stylelint, i18n, dependency guards, and dead-export scans.
- Fresh independent review: no actionable P0–P2 findings.
- Production delta: +5/−10 lines (net −5); regression coverage: +54/−6 lines.
