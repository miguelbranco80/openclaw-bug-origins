## What Problem This Solves

Fixes Quick Chat remaining busy after a completed turn loses its acknowledgement, or sending a queued draft to a replacement Gateway.

## User Impact

Retrying an unchanged draft can recover the completed reply without generating a second provider turn. Incomplete or unrelated history remains an error.

## Why This Change Was Made

Carry the selected Gateway generation through socket dispatch, preserve the original retry key, and recover only its bounded, matching terminal history. The replay preserves current desktop request and sleep ownership checks, including a selected configuration at the actual socket enqueue.

The existing Canvas refresh and widget command interfaces remain unchanged until the next stack layer.
