Fixes #173925.

## What Problem This Solves

Guests with session-write access cannot mark their own conversations unread: the Actions menu disables the item and asks for the broader `operator.write` scope. The same mismatch in the action and automatic-read callers would prevent a complete unread/read cycle even if the menu alone were enabled.

## User Impact

Session writers can use unread reminders on their existing owned conversations from the sidebar, chat header, and sessions list, then mark them read explicitly or by reopening the chat. Foreign conversations remain protected by the existing ownership check; read-only access and unavailable methods remain disabled. Broad-write and administrator behavior is retained.

Manual unread markers keep their existing timestamp protection, and implicit acknowledgement of folded hidden runs remains conditional. The change adds no permission, configuration, schema, or persistence contract.

## Why This Change Was Made

The Gateway already admits ordinary owned-session organization through `operator.sessions.write`. The Control UI permission resolver supports that alternative when a scoped consumer opts in, but unread callers were selecting its broad default. This change passes the existing scope option at those consumers and supplies the existing session ownership fact to automatic acknowledgement.

Other appearance, grouping, assignment, and privileged actions keep their present access requirements. The shared authorization resolver and Gateway remain unchanged.
