Typing at the end of a large, height-capped draft still forces repeated textarea layout in browsers using measured autosizing, even after #186874.

## User Impact

Appending text to an already capped draft keeps its height and scrolling without synchronous autosize or overflow measurements. Desktop and mobile growth limits stay the same.

Reuse the draft that actually proved overflow in the existing sizing owner. Edits that replace or shorten that measured prefix, resize observations, and scheduled layout changes invalidate the result. Native content sizing keeps its existing path. No character-count guess is needed.
