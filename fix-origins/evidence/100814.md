## Fix message body

```text
Repeated bundle-URL scans could trap while transient bundles were being
retired, terminating the process from Quick Chat permission setup. Resolve
this process-stable fact once and recognize known runners before inspecting
bundles. Preserve the pure resolver and its existing fallback contracts.

Normal app permission polling keeps its behavior; test detection no longer
repeatedly walks a changing Foundation bundle inventory. Add concurrent
coverage through the real getter. The original getter traps under the
missing-URL negative control, while the repaired getter remains stable.

Validation: three complete default native replays, 2509 tests each; rendered
chat and 12 named-profile AppState tests; 20 pressured Quick Chat/Nix runs;
old-owner negative control; Swift build/lint/format; Testbox changed checks;
P2 review. The separate named-profile Model menu test times out identically
on the unchanged base and candidate in the macOS VM; no assertion changed.
The new concurrent getter test takes about 1 ms.
```
