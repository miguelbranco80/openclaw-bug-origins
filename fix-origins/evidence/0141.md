## Fix PR #200190 description (by steipete, opened 2026-11-16T17:40:35Z)

```text
## What Problem This Solves

Fixes completed subagent results becoming stranded when a retryable requester completion loses its scheduled wake after cancellation, timeout, preparation invalidation, or conflict.

Related: #200020, #200005, #200165.

## User Impact

Retryable requester completions keep the existing bounded retry schedule. After three attempts, the registry records a terminal failure with the existing visible failure notice instead of leaving a retryable label with no scheduled work. Successful parent `NO_REPLY` or empty settlement remains intentionally quiet.

Already-stranded rows whose older build retired the wake and suppressed delivery require explicit recovery; upgrading does not resurrect those wakes. Runs with committed or unknown effects remain failed for explicit recovery rather than replaying their work. No configuration or schema changes.

## Why This Change Was Made

The delivery classifier already owns retryability, but the scheduler applied a second reason allowlist. Typed timeout and preparation failures could therefore lose their wake. Predispatch cancellation also returned an unclassified `path: none`, while exhausted retryable outcomes retained that disposition in storage.

One typed error map now classifies these interruptions using admission and execution-owned replay-safety facts. The scheduler consumes the disposition directly, and the persistence owner records exhaustion for previously failed/retryable rows. This removes the duplicate allowlist and unbounded preadmission retry branch. Production delta is +39 lines; the growth carries the typed interruption and replay-safety distinctions. Test delta is +154 lines, with +10 documentation lines. Ordinary completion delivery retains its existing frozen result and stable idempotency identity; the stricter replay-safety checks apply to fresh requester-settle attempts.
```
