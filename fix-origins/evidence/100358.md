## What Problem This Solves

A visitor admitted while an invitation is active can retain an open Gateway connection or accepted run after that invitation expires or is revoked. Losing the original email identity or the Visitor Access plugin can also leave previously captured authority usable.

## User Impact

Expiry, revocation and original-identity loss end the captured visitor authority across WebSocket, HTTP, direct tool and OpenAI-compatible requests, including active and queued Gateway runs. Cancellation preserves saved work and joins accepted terminal and partial-transcript writes. Independent staff and Gateway-owner authority remains usable.

Operators can bind the existing guest role to `accessPolicyPlugin: "visitor-access"`. The role then requires that policy even when its manifest is absent or invalid, loading fails, it is disabled, or startup is incomplete. Unbound staff roles and the Gateway owner retain their independent access. Guest admission on older code without this binding is unsupported; recovery keeps the binding and restricted role intact.

## Why This Change Was Made

Requests and accepted runs retain the invitation under which they were admitted. Renewing an active invitation keeps its generation; inviting someone again after expiry creates a new generation and cannot revive ended work. Exact run identity and lifecycle generation keep cancellation from affecting replacement or independent work. A captured invitation also ends when its original email alias moves to another profile; restoring the alias cannot revive it.

The durable invitation becomes active only after confirmed provider membership. Failed or uncertain provider writes keep an inactive cleanup record or preserve the previous deadline. Legacy records require positive membership confirmation before use, while qualified grants retain their local expiry across restart. Native cancellation signals survive plugin retirement and garbage collection while callbacks remain bound to their original instance. Configuration help, SDK documentation and the Visitor Access README describe these contracts.
