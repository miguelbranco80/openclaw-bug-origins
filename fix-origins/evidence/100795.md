Fixes #160431

## What Problem This Solves

Fixes lost Telegram replies when assistant Markdown contains deeply nested `<details>` blocks.

## User Impact

Deep replies retain rich formatting through 15 container levels and preserve deeper text and media source URLs inside the rich message, rather than overflowing the stack. Ordinary details, lists, and inline formatting remain unchanged. No configuration changes or migrations are required.

## Why This Change Was Made

The previous list-only guard ran after recursive rendering, and the original depth constant was unused. Bound HTML parsing and emission before recursive consumers, use iterative measurement and plain-text projection, and normalize rendered and caller-supplied blocks before splitting or serialization. Telegram Test Server accepts 15 nested details containers plus a paragraph leaf, but rejects 16 with `RICH_MESSAGE_DEPTH_INVALID`.

The review follow-up also preserves image, video, and audio source URLs at both early HTML cutoffs. Normal text projection is unchanged. It fixes the content-loss finding rather than merely fixing lint.

Preserves @Ayushdevo's original commit and credit. Thanks to @hpyhandsome for the concrete reproduction.

No overlap with Pash/Sarah changes.
