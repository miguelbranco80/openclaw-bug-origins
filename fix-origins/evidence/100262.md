Related: #169446

## What Problem This Solves

Fixes Git updates losing their original failure and skipping temporary-worktree cleanup when cleanup progress cannot be written to update history.

## User Impact

Cleanup still runs when its progress ledger is unavailable. Operators retain the original failure and secondary diagnostics instead of a misleading cleanup-only failure. Reporting alone does not invalidate a successful update or a known pre-mutation recovery classification; uncertain process cleanup still blocks recovery.

This repairs the installed updater's error handling. It cannot patch an older updater already running, and it does not establish the cause of the reported production maintenance collision or prove full macOS service recovery.

## Why This Change Was Made

Record cleanup reporting failures separately from cleanup execution, preserve both failures during unwind, and keep the original admission/revocation classification only for reporting-only aggregation. Existing process-settlement and recovery guards remain active.

Reported and requested by @hannesrudolph.
