## What Problem This Solves

Fixes: goal controls can stay unconfirmed after a lost reply, while a committed change can be reported as failed when its notification fails.

## User Impact

Operators can explicitly check an unconfirmed Pause, Resume, Clear, or edit after reconnecting or reloading, even when the original goal control disappeared. Nothing resumes automatically. Saved requests expire after 24 hours; forgetting the browser or switching accounts removes the previous account's saved payloads for that Gateway. Controls are not sent or restored when the connection has no distinguishable recovery owner.

## Why This Change Was Made

The existing durable receipt remains the outcome authority. A bounded request and tab-scoped saved identity let the independent recovery notice retry that same operation without creating a second continuation. Definitive precommit rejections leave no armed retry, while unknown outcomes retain their identity. Server-reported receipt expiry strips the saved payload and retains a refresh fence, including in-flight expiry and clock skew. A committed change stays successful despite notification failure, without releasing its event-writer custody early.
