## Fix PR #186040 description (by vyctorbrzezowski, opened 2026-10-27T14:53:30Z)

```text
## What Problem This Solves

Fixes #186038. Completion notifications move into the command palette instead of staying at their normal viewport corner.

## User Impact

Toasts keep the same position with Cmd+K open or closed, while their actions and the palette remain usable.

## Why This Change Was Made

Present the existing toast host as a manual native popover so modal transforms cannot change its positioning reference. Keep the host inside the modal for focus and inertness, and preserve the shell's responsive offset during the handoff.

## Evidence

- Reproduced through background-session completion in the full Control UI with a mocked Gateway: at 1360 × 900, closing the palette moved the toast 310 px horizontally and 120 px vertically. The fix keeps identical open/closed coordinates at 1360 × 900 and 390 × 844, with palette focus, Escape and the Open session action verified.
- Existing toast browser suite: 12 passing tests in Chromium and 12 in WebKit, including modal actions, viewport resizing, placement and narrow layouts. Wall times: 2.56 s and 3.19 s.
- Toast lifecycle and lazy-loading suites: 20 passing tests; 1.49 s wall time.
- UI typecheck, focused lint, stylesheet lint and formatting passed. CI timing is pending.


## Risk and coverage

Changes notification presentation only; it keeps one host and the existing queue, timers and modal policy. No live Gateway was needed for the browser geometry proof.

## Visual evidence

The same background-session completion with Cmd+K open, at 1360 × 900. The toast now stays at its normal viewport corner.
```
