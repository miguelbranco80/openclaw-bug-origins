Fixes unusable model/runtime selections for operators with role restrictions and missing shared-chat draft previews when a connected viewer is absent from the bounded presence cache.

Unsupported runtimes show an availability reason before selection; compatible runtimes remain available. Connected viewers receive shared drafts while authorization, identity deduplication and disconnect checks remain enforced. No configuration migration or protocol-version change is required.
