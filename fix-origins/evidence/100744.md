## What Problem This Solves

Worker workspace recovery can rediscover a different session store after routing changes, redirecting conflict reports or result settlement away from the session that owns the pending work.

## User Impact

Each recovery attempt keeps one exact session source and rechecks its generation and recovery authority. If the source is replaced or authority is revoked, pending results, staged refs, and unfinished journals remain available for a later authorized recovery. Archived sessions can finish retained recovery without becoming active again. Committed destruction still completes its explicit settlement path.

## Why This Change Was Made

The existing recovery owner prepares the source once and carries it through recovery, conflict reporting, and settlement. Initial row preparation runs in the reader worker. Stable revision checks reuse verified facts; changed revisions compare only the protected owner fields through the existing revision and transcript-write owners, retaining live-authority and before/after revision fences. No stored fields, schema, worker protocol, or dependency changes. Related: #150153.
