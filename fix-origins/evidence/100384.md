improve: continue a resumed answer in the block that handed off (#165542) (2026-10-06T05:56:35+01:00, Shakker, 13 files)

Message (truncated):

```text
* improve: continue a resumed answer in the block that handed off

When a session resumed after waiting on subagents, its answer started a
second assistant block with its own clock, its own "Worked for" rollup and
a closing line that covered only the last run.

The resumed run now joins the block that handed off and keeps that row. Its
working line counts from the request and adds the earlier runs' output
tokens, and the closing line reports the whole request, the wait included.
The resumed work stays in place instead of a rollup. A message sent after
the handoff still starts its own block.

* fix: keep a handed-off block in place until it resumes

Between the last subagent finishing and the resumed run starting, the
handed-off turn had nothing live in it, so its operations were rolled up
under a "Worked" header above the handoff sentence and unfolded again on
resume.

Every confirmed handoff now keeps its structural boundary, resumed or not,
and a turn that holds one is never rolled up.

* docs: say a handed-off turn keeps its work in place

The chat guide still described the rollup exception as an answer that
follows a handoff. A turn that handed off stays in place whether it is
waiting, has resumed or never did, and shows no working indicator between
its last subagent finishing and its resume.

* fix: keep operations on either side of a handoff in one row

A resumed run that went straight back to work left two operation rows
stacked in the block: the handed-off run's and its own, with nothing
between them. No row marks a handoff, so the block now joins the row
after one to the row before it. The joined row keeps the earlier row's
identity, so what the reader opened while waiting stays open.

Operations stay separate rows when the agent wrote something between
them.
```
