Cancellation now carries the bounded, redacted active phase/path, elapsed time, progress age and copy counters through the existing failure formatter, the durable receipt and openclaw update status, and the size label states the total estimated snapshot input. Fixes #177225

## What Problem This Solves

Fixes #177225. Managed-update snapshot watchdog failures could discard the last phase/path and leave the durable failure's stderr empty.

## User Impact

Snapshot failures retain the active phase/source, elapsed time, last progress age, and last database's page/byte counters in the error, saved receipt, and `openclaw update status`. The watchdog now labels its bytes as **total estimated snapshot input, including SQLite and plugins**, rather than implying that one database of that size was being copied.

This repairs the installed updater going forward; a newer staged candidate cannot restore diagnostics already discarded by an older updater. No options, schemas, dependencies, or activation policy change.

## Why This Change Was Made

The snapshot supervisor checked cancellation before calling the existing failure formatter, after parsed progress had already been removed from stderr. Phase-only progress was also excluded from receipts. Watchdog cancellation now uses the ordinary-failure formatter after worker settlement, including cancellation while a completed worker's progress receipt drains. The parser preserves the last database counters when a later phase-only event arrives; the canary and receipt/report projections retain the resulting stderr. Process-tree settlement and uncertain-cleanup handling stay intact.

The reported stall matches the sequence in #176813/#176968 (`1d210bb5`): the parent's progress writer could retry a synchronous shared-state authorization snapshot without limit after the child's shared copy completed. That repair already bounds admission attempts and warns when optional progress recording is skipped. The cause of #177225's Raspberry Pi stall remains **unproven** because its report lacks the repeated-worker/write evidence needed to establish identity. This change preserves the earlier repair and covers both a pending progress receipt after the shared copy and a later plugin-inventory stall. Phase-only forwarding overlaps the narrower work in #163463; cancellation formatting and durable stderr complete the repair here.
