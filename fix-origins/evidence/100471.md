## What Problem This Solves

Fixes the literal ellipsis shown in “Waiting for turn to start…” while a chat turn is queued, restoring the punctuation cleanup already applied to the response-wait label.

## User Impact

Queued turns show “Waiting for turn to start” without the trailing ellipsis, matching the response-wait label. Queue behavior, elapsed time, and other startup labels are unchanged.

Correct the canonical English label and update existing test expectations, with one regression assertion rejecting the ellipsis. Generated translations and locale metadata are intentionally unchanged; the normal post-merge locale workflow owns their refresh.
