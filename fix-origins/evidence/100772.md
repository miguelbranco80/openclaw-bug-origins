## Fix message body

```text
A late identical watcher observation could pause a restart after its target
was published, then be consumed without reaccepting the paused restart.
Bind acceptance to its captured source epoch and reaccept verified echoes
before settling the baseline, while keeping the audit write single-shot.

Isolate the writer-owned test watcher and exercise readiness arriving during
acceptance with the original secrets and emission assertions. Move options
and the existing environment fixture into their existing support modules
and retire the now-private prepared-candidate type export.

Proof: two failing original-code controls; 20 repaired whole-file runs;
three original CI shard replays; reload/restart siblings; a final 115-test
replay after explicit epoch assignment; all selected changed-file guards.
Whole-file pnpm repetitions took 48-49 seconds; final Vitest time was 45.75s.

Follow-up to #161749.
```
