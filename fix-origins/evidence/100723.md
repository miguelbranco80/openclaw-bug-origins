```text
## What Problem This Solves

Fixes redundant database snapshot work while the updater waits for candidate readiness with a cold recovery reader and no startup warning.

## User Impact

Future updater runs perform fewer blocking snapshot children in this readiness path. Four synthetic probes require 8 snapshots instead of 16. This does not patch an already-running or older installed updater, and the measurements are not a whole-upgrade speedup claim.

## Why This Change Was Made

The readiness owner awaited an already-resolved placeholder warning promise twice per probe, then repeated the recovery/authority check. A cold recovery read can launch a synchronous SQLite snapshot child.

Represent absent warning work explicitly and await it only once a real warning exists. Checks still bracket every async probe; warning settlement, cancellation, generation/custody checks, leases, rollback, and exact-version verification retain their existing behavior. No workers, concurrency, caches, schema/config changes, or timeout increases were added. Production delta: +5 lines; tests: +108.
