## Fix PR #163352 description (by steipete, opened 2026-10-02T07:16:12Z)

```text
## What Problem This Solves

Fixes: when a completed subagent (for example a visible child in a managed worktree) is given its next turn before its previous result has been announced, the previous turn's announcement to the parent is dropped. The log shows `Subagent announce failed: ... transcript identity changed during announcement.`, and the parent only ever hears about the later turn.

## User Impact

Each completed turn keeps its own parent announcement and receipt while the child continues working, so parents no longer miss results from quick follow-ups. Child transcripts were never affected.

## Why This Change Was Made

Ordinary follow-up admission in `reactivateCompletedSubagentSession` went through steer replacement, which retired the completed run's registry row along with its pending delivery owner. Follow-ups now preserve the completed execution through the registry row owner (`mutateSubagentRuns`). A superseded run that still owes its completion is announced instead of retired, and the new turn gets independent successor authority. Delivery stays bound to the exact execution, and the predecessor's session effects stay fenced from the newer turn. Paused continuation and steer keep their replacement behavior. No schema change. Production LOC +96/-65.

## Evidence

- Found by a live run on an isolated Gateway with a real model: in the managed-worktree scenario, turn 2 was admitted about 1 s after turn 1 completed, and turn 1's parent announcement was never delivered. The same loss shows up in stopped databases from builds before the registry redesign (#163195), so this is pre-existing.
- The regression drives registered agent admission with paused transcript-read and delivery-admission boundaries, distinct per-turn outputs, durable receipts, restored pending delivery, and replay deduplication. It fails on main and passes for all three interleavings (4.66 s, 2.85 s, 2.39 s).
```
