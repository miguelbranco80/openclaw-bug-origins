```text
## What Problem This Solves

Fixes members being able to archive threads created by someone else when they have general write access.

## User Impact

Members can archive and restore only threads they created; admins retain access across creators. Assigned ownership alone no longer grants archive or restore access, including for existing sessions after an upgrade. This is the requested policy change; the multi-user guide documents admin recovery when the creator is unavailable or lacks usable profile attribution. Other collaboration actions, assigned-owner stop controls, and solo gateways retain their existing behavior. No configuration or migration is required.

## Why This Change Was Made

Enforce creator/admin authority at the shared Gateway authorization boundary for single and batch patches, including checks after asynchronous work. Align the UI and agent controls so an unauthorized self-archive cannot report success before being rejected. The conflict resolution preserves main's newer session-assignment support.
```
