## What Problem This Solves

Fixes: one `transcripts` list call can walk the entire archive when most or all meetings are inaccessible to the caller.

## User Impact

Each call examines at most 200 stored meetings. Empty and partial pages explain how to continue, so authorized older meetings remain discoverable. Existing authorization and output limits remain in effect. Offsets can shift when captures are added or deleted between calls; restart listing to refresh.

## Why This Change Was Made

Use the existing `TRANSCRIPTS_LIST_MAX` at the tool's pagination owner and return `nextOffset` after every consumed row, including denied rows. This removes the unbounded automatic page walk and its repeated hard-coded page size. The necessary continuation argument, result, and instructions account for the net +10 production lines; no new budget constant or state owner is introduced.
