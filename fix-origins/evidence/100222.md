## What Problem This Solves

Fixes an issue where users lose the archive Undo toast after six seconds even while hovering over it or focusing its action with the keyboard.

## Why This Change Was Made

The shared toast owner now pauses its existing expiry timer instead of making every caller manage interaction timing. Focus transfers between toast controls stay paused; explicit actions/dismissal, replacement, FIFO ordering, and modal relocation remain supported. True disconnection clears queued outcomes and timers. Existing archive Undo is unchanged; no new undo mechanism, persistent notification, stacking redesign, or global setting is introduced.

## User Impact

Users can pause to read and activate Undo without losing the action or their focus to an automatic timeout. The toast resumes its remaining lifetime only after both pointer hover and keyboard focus leave. No migration or settings changes.

Related: #128270 is the broader operator-notice presentation work. This PR is limited to the current shared toast’s interaction-aware expiry and its regressions; it does not take over that redesign.
