## Fix PR #200967 description (by steipete, opened 2026-11-18T03:33:41Z)

```text
## What Problem This Solves

Finished subagent results can remain pending indefinitely when a yielded batch also contains children paused to wait for a message. Restart recovery restores the owed results, but the batch never attempts delivery.

## User Impact

Ready results can reach the requester or settle through the existing visible permanent-failure path. Message-waiting children remain resumable and retain their future completion; saved child results remain readable. No expiry, schema, or protocol changes.

## Why This Change Was Made

The frozen-batch gate in `src/agents/subagents/announce/subagent-announce.requester-settle-wake.ts:165` on the base required every surviving member to pass `isRequesterSettleMemberReady`, which always rejects `sessions_yield`. An ended message wait therefore blocked every ready sibling without a dispatch attempt. The killed member already follows the intentional non-delivery path.

Keep the existing dispatch owner and select the ready members once every other member is an ended message wait. The lifecycle failure fallback (`subagent-registry-lifecycle-wake.ts:338`) must exclude those waits too, so a preparation failure cannot consume their future wakes. Both paths share the liveness owner's predicate. Running children and pauses waiting on descendants still hold the batch. Overlapping cohorts retain the existing changed-batch permanent-failure handling and saved result payloads.

The change was rebased onto main after #200924 merged before editing the shared dispatch file. The simplification pass retained the two existing settlement boundaries and one integration regression; no new owner, timer, or policy was added. Production delta: +29/-13 lines; regression: +213 lines; docs: +4/-2 lines.

## Evidence

The regression models 89 restored rows, 68 distinct members in overlapping 33-member batches, the success/error/no-outcome mix, prior deliveries and unavailable sinks, nine message waits, and a killed child. It exercises real SQLite persistence/restore, restart custody, the sweeper, lifecycle dispatch, descendant checks, and durable settlement, with fixture session I/O and the external delivery sink replaced.

Red on base `38439333e1f49667c8fc7fe572a10637f36af211`: the first sweep fails with `expected "vi.fn()" to be called at least once`; the owed batch never reaches delivery. The same regression is green with the fix: ready rows clear their wakes through delivery or recorded permanent failure, saved results remain readable, and all nine message waits keep their wakes.
```
