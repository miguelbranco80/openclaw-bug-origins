## Fix PR #164561 description (by steipete, opened 2026-10-03T21:42:20Z)

```text
## What Problem This Solves

Legacy or unauthenticated runtime-context entries can leak into compaction summaries and saved CLI session notes when their persisted rows lack producer metadata.

## User Impact

Reserved `openclaw.runtime-context` entries remain private when older transcripts are read, including entries without carrier metadata and entries explicitly opted out of provider replay. Conversation and durable-note retention rules stay intact. Stored history is not rewritten and no migration is needed.

## Why This Change Was Made

The runtime-context cutover made the privacy classifier use the stricter provider-carrier recognizer. That conflated hiding private content with granting provider replay authority. This is fail-closed hiding: reserved runtime-context custom messages stay private even when they have no producer or replay authority; the separate provider-authority checks remain strict. It protects persisted transcripts from before the cutover as well as unauthenticated entries.

Main now seeds CLI history through the canonical producer (`f40a007daffa`) and checks typed completion events in `internalEvents` (`31412680307a`). This rebase takes both peer-fixed test files unchanged from main. The remaining change is the privacy classifier, its token-sanitization regression, and the transcript-hygiene documentation.
```
