## What Problem This Solves

Pressing End in the transcript while tool results stream can stop thousands of pixels above the bottom. The full overlapping-stream reproduction on the base leaves Chromium 7,250 px short (82,430 / 89,680).

## User Impact

Plain End on the transcript uses the same manual follow action as Latest. Nested scrollports, editable targets, modified keys, and already-cancelled input retain native handling.

## Why This Change Was Made

The pane already owns manual follow intent. Route admitted End to that owner and prevent the competing native default. Also renew its existing end destination when the stream grows after virtual-core's five-second scroll journey expires, without restarting prepared rows or introducing another timer/state owner.

The earlier routed-only prototype already prevented default, so the proposed uncancelled-native-animation explanation does **not** explain its historical 4,428 px miss. That exact miss did not recur in detailed, light, minimal or pre-lookup replays; its writer remains unproven. The expired-journey gap is separately reproduced by the new regression test, red on base and green here.
