## What Problem This Solves

Fixes Settings losing edits entered after Discard when an earlier autosave is still pending.

## User Impact

Newer Raw edits remain available to save, and newer Form edits resume autosaving after the pending request settles.

## Why This Change Was Made

At `ui/src/lib/config/config-draft-discard.ts:19`, Discard drained the outstanding write and then replaced the draft without checking for newer editing intent. The existing write coordinator now tracks that intent, abandons a superseded discard before clearing patches, and reuses the configuration read owner's admission callback to guard the reload response. Autosave resumes when a newer edit supersedes Discard.

No parallel discard owner or recovery path was added. Production delta: +20/-6; tests: +47/-0; documentation: +1/-1.
