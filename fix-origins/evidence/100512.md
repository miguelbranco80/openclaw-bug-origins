## What Problem This Solves

Fixes the stuck highlight and assignee submenu when the mouse moves from a tall user list onto another parent-menu row.

## User Impact

Sidebar and chat-header menus now close the old submenu and highlight the row under the pointer. Moving across another parent row deliberately targets that row instead of preserving the previous branch through an invisible hover corridor. Direct submenu entry, paced edge-to-choice diagonal movement, and keyboard selection remain covered.

## Why This Change Was Made

Disable hit testing only on the shared submenu safe-triangle pseudo-element. The submenu itself stays interactive, and Web Awesome remains the single owner of visibility, focus, closing grace, and branch retirement. No dependency patch or second hover controller is added.
